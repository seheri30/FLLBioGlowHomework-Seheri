# FLLBioGlowHomework-Seheri
Cruise Navigation Homework
This is my navigation map for the robot cruise trip.
How my map works
I drew a long path made of small rectangles.
Each rectangle has an arrow to show the direction the cruise should move.
The cruise travels through the path and stops at the islands in this order:

Island A
Island B
Island C

There are curves, straight paths, and a continuation section that leads to the final End point.
Sequence of Steps:

First, the cruise starts at the “Start” point and follows the arrows forward.
Next, it goes straight and reaches Island A.
Then, it continues along a long straight path, then makes a sharp right turn and moves one step forward. After that, it makes another sharp right turn and moves forward to reach Island B.
Next, it moves forward a few steps, then turns left and moves one step forward. It turns left again and continues moving forward. Then it makes a sharp right turn, followed by another sharp right turn, and moves forward following the arrows.
After that, it turns left, goes straight, then turns left again. It makes a sharp left turn and moves forward. Then it turns right and moves a few steps, turns right again and moves a few steps, and finally turns left to reach Island C.
Finally, the cruise moves forward a few more steps and reaches the End point.



What I learned

How to plan steps in order (sequence).
How to guide a robot by drawing arrows.
How to show turns, curves, and direction.
How the robot can follow a map to reach islands one by one.
